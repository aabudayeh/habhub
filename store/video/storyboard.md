# HabHub video masters

The three still-based generated masters use release-candidate HabHub screens
captured from the fresh credential-free demo. Captions, crops, a brand
background and short fades are the only additions. Sentence-case headlines
lead with one benefit, category labels provide context, and readable demo
disclosures stay in the footer. Headline and caption heights are measured so
the app never covers the message. Names, entries and progress
imagery are synthetic. The separate continuous interactive guide is described
below and must not be represented as the same kind of montage.

Master: 886 × 1920 portrait, H.264 video with AAC audio, 30 fps.

## Comprehensive feature tour — 102 seconds

The long-form tour is a separate 1080 × 1920 master, not an App Store preview.
Thirty-three real-screen beats plus the brand outro run for three seconds each.
Subtle motion keeps the capture alive; branded action prompts sit in reserved
space above the app on interaction beats. They suggest the next action while
keeping the real controls readable; the continuous guide shows those actions
being performed. Short captions support a sound-off viewing experience.

1. Start with Today.
2. Personalize the real Today page with live setup; skip tips at any time.
3. Enter edit mode; pin, hide and reorder.
4. Keep goals and to-dos independent.
5. Paste nested to-dos and `#labels` as a batch.
6. Stage the batch, inspect nested Sub-To-Dos, then decide whether to save.
7. Open any tracker and its entries.
8. Read line and bar history views.
9. Duplicate a tracker interaction style.
10. Compare nutrition logs, daily references and personal goals.
11. Start a stopwatch or countdown.
12. Save timed activity as a named workout with distance and energy.
13. Build or reuse a workout plan.
14. Complete live sets and rests.
15. Review average set load and exercise progress.
16. Read the progress grid.
17. Review the five-photo synthetic timeline.
18. Compare and collage synthetic progress photos.
19. View the evolving avatar and body profile.
20. Compare group progress with privacy intact.
21. Run a challenge with selected participants.
22. Replay recap stories linked to the feed and open the comment composer.
23. Add a shared group event.
24. Keep a social group note.
25. Use group/direct chat and message reactions.
26. Review badges and milestones.
27. Use the optional personal schedule.
28. Link rich journal notes to the story behind the data.
29. Choose notification categories and quiet hours.
30. Tune display, page order and navigation.
31. Understand opt-in food-goal and unrecorded-step behavior.
32. Learn page by page in Watch or Practice mode.
33. Find optional power in the uncluttered menu.
34. HabHub outro.

## Full interactive Watch guide — continuous live capture

Output:
`store/exports/video/interactive-guide/en-US/habhub-full-interactive-guide-1080x1920.mp4`

This fourth master begins on the real Quick Guide page and visibly starts with
the actual `Watch Complete HabHub guide` control. From there it preserves the
app's own timing, animated pointer, automatic page transitions, and isolated
tutorial actions through the final full-guide step. It is recorded continuously
from a clean credential-free synthetic demo in Edge/CDP, then scaled to
1080 × 1920 and paired with an intentionally silent AAC track.

This is the reproducible how-to walkthrough and the evidence that Watch mode
can traverse the release candidate; it is not a still montage and it is not an
Apple app preview. Any later branded intro, voiceover, music, or native-device
insert must be versioned as a derivative without replacing this uncut master.

## Truth gates

- The Today capture must show the compact default navigation. Schedule,
  Journal and Performance remain optional and are introduced through the menu.
- Progress photos are always labelled as synthetic demo imagery.
- Background health import, notification delivery/taps, Android widgets,
  Android progress-video export and Apple Health permission flows require
  signed-device footage before any of those behaviors enter a public video.
- Do not promise exact background timing, Samsung-exclusive data, calorie
  accuracy, a weight-loss rate or a medical outcome.
