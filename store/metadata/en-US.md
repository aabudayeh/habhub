### Description

Your day. Your progress. Your people.

HabHub brings your habits, goals, workouts and to-dos into one personal home.
Start with what matters today. Add more when you need it.

MAKE TODAY YOURS
Choose the trackers you want to see, set a target and make a quick check-in.
Pin, hide or rearrange your daily view. Prefer goals, to-dos or both? You decide.
Paste a list with nested bullets to build tasks and subtasks in one go.

TRACK MORE THAN A NUMBER
Follow steps, water, study time or a goal of your own. Reuse a tracker style,
choose a unit and tune the way you log. Explore dated entries, line and bar
charts, calendar views and progress grids to see your patterns over time.

KEEP YOUR WORKOUT TOGETHER
Build a plan or use a template. Log exercises, sets and rests, time an activity,
and review your workout and exercise history. Optional distance and energy
entries keep your activity logs connected.

SEE THE WHOLE PICTURE
Compare progress photos and make a collage. Keep journal notes beside the
numbers. Plan your routine with schedules and reminder controls, and keep
earned badges and milestones in view.

BUILD MOMENTUM WITH FRIENDS
Create a private group, set a shared challenge and compare the goals each
person chooses to share. Cheer a recap, react to a message, or continue the
conversation in chat. Shared notes and a group schedule keep plans easy to find.
Tracker visibility controls let you choose which details stay private.

LEARN AS YOU MAKE IT YOURS
Choose your interests, then personalize the real Today page as you explore.
Prefer a checklist? Classic setup is still available. Try optional Watch or
Practice guides with safe demo data, or skip all automatic tutorials at once.
Advanced customisation stays available when you are ready for it.

HabHub is a self-tracking tool, not a medical device or medical-advice service.
Connected services, data types, and background timing vary by platform,
permission, and operating-system conditions.

## Creative direction

Lead with one human benefit per frame: a personal day, visible progress, or
shared momentum. The first screenshot introduces Today; the next two show a
tracker's history and the progress grid. Keep feature detail in the product
tour and the longer description so the first impression remains simple.

Use sentence-case headlines, no more than two lines, a short supporting
sentence, and one dominant real-app capture. The compact category label helps
people understand where the feature belongs. Keep demo disclosures readable
at the bottom without turning them into the main marketing message. Never
add ratings, testimonials, download counts or health-result promises.

## Claims deliberately excluded

- guaranteed weight loss, calorie accuracy, or health outcomes
- continuous, exact, or Samsung-exclusive background synchronization
- iOS home-screen widgets
- iOS native progress-video export
- medical diagnosis, treatment, or clinical-grade body measurements
