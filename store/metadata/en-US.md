### Description

HabHub turns the goals you care about into one flexible daily home.

Build your Today page around custom trackers and to-dos. Log a quick check-in,
open any tracker to explore its history, and use progress maps, charts, photos,
workout history, journal notes, schedules, and reminders to understand what is
actually helping.

When you want accountability, create a private group with friends. Compare the
goals you choose to share, take part in challenges, celebrate badges, and keep
the conversation in one place. Visibility controls let each tracker stay private
or appear in the group experiences you permit.

Highlights:

- A customizable Today dashboard for goals and to-dos
- Flexible trackers with targets, formulas, notes, and history charts
- Calendar and grid-map progress views
- Progress-photo comparison and collage tools
- Workout plans, live sets, timers, and saved history
- Private groups, leaderboards, challenges, badges, and chat
- Schedules, reminders, journal notes, and recaps
- Optional read-only connected-health imports on supported devices

HabHub is a self-tracking tool, not a medical device or medical-advice service.
Connected services, data types, and background timing vary by platform,
permission, and operating-system conditions.
